# 🧬 Biological Anatomy & Locomotion Kinematic Guidelines

Har animal ko generate karne se pehle uski **asli biological taxonomy aur joint physics research** ko follow karna zaroori hai:

---

## 1. 🐕 Quadrupeds (Dogs, Wolves, Big Cats, Horses):
* **Forelimbs (Front Hands/Legs):**
  * Shoulder (Scapula) $\rightarrow$ Upper Arm (Humerus)
  * **Elbow Joint:** Humesha **PEECHHE (Caudal)** ki taraf bend hoga ribs ke paas.
  * Forearm (Radius/Ulna) $\rightarrow$ Carpus wrist pastern $\rightarrow$ 4-Toe Digitigrade Paw.
* **Hindlimbs (Back Legs — 3-Segment Kinematics):**
  * Pelvis (Hip) $\rightarrow$ Thigh (Femur)
  * **Stifle (True Knee):** Humesha **AAGE (Forward / Cranial)** ki taraf bend hoga.
  * **Hock (Ankle / Tarsus):** Humesha **PEECHHE (Backward / Caudal)** ki taraf bend hoga.
  * Metatarsus (Rear Pastern) $\rightarrow$ 4-Toe Paw.
* **Locomotion:** Diagonal 2-Beat Trotting Gait ($FL+HR$ vs $FR+HL$).
* **Face & Tail:** Muzzle mask, wet leather nose, floppy/erect ears with pink inner shading, glossy eyes with highlights, harmonic spring wagging tail.

---

## 2. 🦂 Arachnids (Scorpions, Tarantulas):
* **Limbs (8 Legs):** 4 Left & 4 Right with Coxa $\rightarrow$ Femur $\rightarrow$ Tibia $\rightarrow$ Tarsal claws (Tripod stepping gait).
* **Pedipalp Arms (Hands):** 3-Joint Ball & Socket ($Shoulder \rightarrow Humerus \rightarrow Brachium \rightarrow Palm \rightarrow Snapping\ Dactylus\ Claws$).
* **Body & Tail:** Prosoma Carapace (Ocular tubercle eyes) $\rightarrow$ 7 Mesosoma Chitin Plates $\rightarrow$ 5 Metasoma Tail Segments $\rightarrow$ Telson Venom Bulb & Needle Stinger.

---

## 3. 🐍 Serpents (Cobras, Black Mambas, Vipers):
* **Head:** Diamond sculpted skull + lateral slit eyes + red forked flicking tongue.
* **Cervical Hood:** Flaring hood ribs behind head.
* **Locomotion:** 48-Vertebrae Lateral Undulation Wave Propagation ($sin(time \cdot \omega + i \cdot k)$).

---

## 4. 🦗 Insects & Crustaceans (Praying Mantis, Mantis Shrimp, Beetles):
* **Head:** Triangular mobile head + dual hemispherical compound dome eyes + antennae.
* **Raptorial Forearms:** Spiked femur + folding razor tibia blade that snaps shut on prey.
* **Walking Legs:** 6 armored slender limbs with alternating tripod insectoid stepping wave.

---

## 5. 🦎 Reptiles (Komodo Dragons, Crocodiles, Chameleons):
* **Skull:** Sculpted reptile skull + dual nostrils + prominent lateral eyes.
* **Limbs:** 4 Sprawling 2-joint limbs with 5-digit spread claws.
* **Spine & Tail:** Undulating S-curve swimming spine with triangular dorsal osteoderm scutes + whip tail.

---

## 6. 🐙 Cephalopods (Octopuses, Squids):
* **Mantle:** Domed muscular mantle + horizontal slit eyes.
* **Limbs:** 8 Independent multi-segment curling tentacles with suction cup rings.

---

## 7. 🦈 Aquatic (Sharks, Manta Rays, Fish):
* **Pectoral Fins:** Broad undulating wing fins with fluid sinusoidal waves.
* **Spine & Tail:** Flexible lateral swimming spine + heterocercal caudal fin.
